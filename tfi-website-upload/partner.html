Collaboration, not duplication

We reach the people who need us most by partnering with the organizations they already trust — extending impact and stretching every dollar further.

Who we work with

Three ways to build something together

Community nonprofits

Already have engaged participants? We bring the fitness-and-nutrition piece to your members, on-site or nearby — no new infrastructure required.

Healthcare providers

Refer patients who would benefit from supervised, adaptive exercise and nutrition support as part of managing chronic conditions.

Funders & grantors

Invest in a lean, community-rooted model with measurable reach into vulnerable populations — and programming shaped by the people it serves.

Why fund TFI

A high-impact investment in community health

  • Addresses the modifiable drivers of chronic disease — physical inactivity and poor nutrition
  • Aligns directly with the NYS Prevention Agenda's goals on healthy eating, active living, and health equity
  • Reaches economically disadvantaged residents through trusted, established partners
  • Designed with participants, from listening sessions to program delivery
  • Led by an exercise specialist and a board-certified physician
