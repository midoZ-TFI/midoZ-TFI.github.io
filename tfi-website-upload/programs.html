Youth & family

Fitness 101 — "Fitness is a family function"

Fitness 101 is a twelve-week childhood-obesity program that involves parent or guardian and child together, because the best results come when the whole family learns new habits side by side.

  • Two one-hour fitness classes each week — cardiovascular, balance, and strength training in a fun, non-competitive setting
  • A weekly half-hour interactive nutrition class with hands-on food-journal review
  • Progress tracked confidentially through simple body measurements and step goals

The goal is a foundation for healthy living that families carry with them long after they leave the gym.
